# PhAST Code of Conduct

## Purpose

PhAST welcomes researchers, students, educators, scientific-software
developers, and users. Participation should support careful technical inquiry,
reproducible research, and an environment in which newcomers can ask basic
questions without dismissal.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation choices rather than personal
  attributes.
- Give technically specific feedback and distinguish errors from differences
  in modelling assumptions.
- State uncertainty, capability boundaries, and missing validation openly.
- Respect attribution, licences, confidentiality, and unpublished research.
- Help contributors reduce a problem to a reproducible command or example.
- Accept correction and revise claims when stronger evidence becomes
  available.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate
misrepresentation, publication of private information, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Technical disagreement does
not justify hostile or demeaning communication.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project
discussions, documentation contributions, and public events where a person is
representing PhAST or CEMS Lab.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact route shown on the maintainer profile or CEMS Lab page. Do not include
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review the
available evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and may edit, hide, close,
or restrict participation when required to protect the community.

## Enforcement

Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. They may
include a clarification request, a warning, removal of content, temporary
participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
Good-faith technical criticism, replication attempts, and reports of numerical
limitations are explicitly welcome.
