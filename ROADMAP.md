# PhAST Roadmap

PhAST's public roadmap tracks solver capabilities intended for the CEMS-Lab
release. Experimental inverse, hybrid, and paper-specific work remains private
until it is validated and promoted through the public example contract.

## Short Term

- Harden YAML and fluent workflow parity for curated public examples.
- Expand installation, validation, result-loading, and postprocessing smoke tests.
- Promote additional validated 2D fracture and solid-mechanics examples with
  standard visual manifests.
- Improve public postprocessing tools for animations, response curves, and
  reproducibility manifests.

## Medium Term

- Promote additional plasticity, cohesive-interface, and PF-CZM workflows after
  beta validation.
- Add stronger mesh, region, material, and boundary-condition authoring helpers.
- Publish reproducible benchmark artifacts with documented hardware and backend
  notes.
- Improve optional PETSc, MUMPS, and cuDSS backend documentation and validation.

## Long Term

- 3D finite-element and fracture workflows.
- Multi-GPU and domain-decomposition scaling.
- Larger validated material libraries and cohesive/interface model coverage.
- Versioned external datasets on archival storage rather than heavy stores in git.
